# NuRobots

### Automate the boring stuff.

{: .note }
> This documentation was written with the help of AI and reviewed by the NuRobots developer because frankly I cannot stand the mindnumbing work of writing documentation lol. If something here doesn't match what you see in game, please let us know.

NuRobots adds robots that work for your players. They mine, farm, chop trees, fish, hunt, ranch and collect items, level up, learn skills and keep working while their owner is offline. Everything from what a robot drops to what it looks like is set in config.

## Features

- **Paper and Folia**, 1.21.11 and newer
- **Virtual and physical robots.** Virtual robots roll a loot table and work anywhere, even offline and in unloaded chunks. Physical robots really break blocks, harvest crops, fell trees and fight mobs around them.
- **Progression.** Levels, experience, skills, fuel, durability, repairs and upgrades.
- **Auto sell** through Vault, with per-item prices, taxes and sell bonuses.
- **Cross-server** with MySQL, MariaDB or PostgreSQL plus Redis. SQLite works out of the box for a single server.
- **Custom looks.** Item and block displays, armor stands with armour and poses, any vanilla mob, ModelEngine models with animations, and MythicMobs mobs.
- **Custom items** from Nexo, Oraxen and ItemsAdder, in loot, menus, equipment and filters.
- **WorldGuard** protection with two extra region flags.
- **PlaceholderAPI and MiniPlaceholders**, both fully supported.
- **Menus for everything**: shop, robot list, details, storage, skills, filter, trusted players.
- **Developer API** with events and custom skill effects.
